3. SMS Messaging — Phone Number Collection, Consent, and Data Restrictions

SMS & Mobile Data Policy

How we obtain consent: When you provide your mobile phone number during the reservation booking process on the Velvet Rope platform, you are presented with a clear opt-in disclosure at the point of submission. The disclosure states:

"By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS notifications about your reservation from Velvet Rope. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Message and data rates may apply."

What we send: We use your mobile phone number solely to send transactional SMS messages directly related to your reservation, including:

  • Reservation confirmations and booking updates
  • Deposit receipts and payment confirmations
  • Check-in reminders and arrival instructions
  • Table status and event notifications

Opt-out: You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Upon receiving STOP, we will send a single confirmation message and immediately cease all further SMS messages to your number. To re-subscribe, reply START.

Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at support@velvetropehq.com. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies, typically 1–5 messages per reservation event.

No sharing of mobile opt-in data: Your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in status, and consent data will not be shared, sold, rented, leased, or transferred to any third parties or affiliates for their marketing, promotional, or advertising purposes. This restriction applies absolutely and without exception.

Your consent to receive SMS messages is not required as a condition of making a reservation or using the Service.

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Reservation data is typically retained for 3 years. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at support@velvetropehq.com.
